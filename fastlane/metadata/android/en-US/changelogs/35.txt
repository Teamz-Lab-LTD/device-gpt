v3.1.16 — Honest app power reporting

- "App Power Monitor" is now "App Screen Time". Android does not report exact battery use per app, so we now show what we can really measure: how long you use each app. No more per-app battery percentages.
- Device power in watts and the component breakdown (display, cellular, RAM, Wi-Fi) are unchanged — those are real measurements.
- Fixed: the day-after overnight battery reminder sometimes never arrived.
