# Keyword Research Prompt for ChatGPT Deep Research (Reusable)

**Use in:** ChatGPT (with Deep Research / web search enabled) to get primary keyword, secondary keywords, FAQ-style keywords, and short justification for a landing page that ranks and sends traffic to your app store listing.

Copy-paste the block below into ChatGPT. Replace the placeholders with your app name, category, and your full extraction output (from the SEO audit or your own extraction).

---

I have a mobile app and I've extracted all its features and user-facing elements. Below is the full list. I need keyword research for a landing page that will rank well and send traffic to my app store listing.

**App name:** [Your app name]

**Category / one-line description:** [e.g. "Habit tracker and daily reminder app for Android"]

**Extracted features, screens, and keyword seeds (from my codebase):**
[PASTE THE FULL EXTRACTION OUTPUT HERE – at least "Features & capabilities", "Screens & flows", "Technical differentiators", and "Raw keyword seeds".]

**What I need from Deep Research:**
1. **Primary keyword:** One main phrase (2–4 words) that has strong search volume and is relevant to my app. Prefer "high intent" (e.g. "[category] app", "best [category] app").
2. **Secondary keywords:** 10–15 phrases that:
   - Are derived from my extracted features and seeds above (so I don't miss feature-based keywords).
   - Have meaningful search volume (prioritize high volume where possible).
   - Are relatively lower competition (so I can realistically rank – prioritize "medium" or "low" difficulty if data is available).
   - Mix: head terms, long-tail, and question-style (for FAQ).
3. **FAQ-style keywords:** 5–10 exact questions people search (e.g. "Does [X] work offline?", "Is [Y] free?") that match my app's features – again prefer decent volume and lower competition.
4. **Short justification:** For primary keyword and top 3 secondary: why this volume/competition balance is good for a new or small app landing page.

Use web search / keyword tools to validate volume and competition where possible. Output in a structured, copy-paste format: Primary keyword, Secondary keywords (list), FAQ keywords (list), then Justification.
