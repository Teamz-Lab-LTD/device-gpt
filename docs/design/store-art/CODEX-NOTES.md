# Decisions and honest limitations

- The brief assigns hero to shots 1–2 and left to 3–4, but later requires that no neighbouring shots use the same composition. These cannot both hold. The final sequence is **hero, left, right, left, right, left, right, left**, prioritising the explicit final visual check and the requested alternating rhythm. The exact supplied headlines and raw-file mapping remain unchanged.
- The display and grid captures are full-screen tests without a status bar. Cropping 120 px from those would remove real instruction text. Their screen crops begin at y=0; the other captures begin at y=120. All end at y=2902. Each rectangle is recorded in screenshots/kry/README.md.
- Pillow in this environment reports Raqm unavailable. All new text is instead rendered through the available `/opt/homebrew/bin/rsvg-convert`, with Pango shaping Kohinoor Bangla. The Bangla headline words and badge were compared visually against the supplied strings in the full-size final PNGs. No detached vowel signs or missing-glyph boxes were seen.
- Screens and enlarged focus cards are literal crops from the original PNGs, resampled once at their output sizes. They are not generated, repainted, recoloured, sharpened, or otherwise retouched. The cards enlarge content by 1.7× or 2× relative to its scale inside the phone; all are built directly from the full-resolution raw capture. Connector lines stay outside captured screen pixels, and the intentionally overlapping cards sit above the phone as required.
- To preserve every retained source pixel, screen crops have square corners inside the rounded device body. The device body has a 14 px border and rounded exterior; no screen-corner artwork or invented notch is added.
- The raw camera screenshot still contains its emulator warning and synthetic camera thumbnails, the grid remains a plain grid, and shot 8 still shows the entry to a report with the existing 9/10 card. No actual report or better camera result was invented. Replace these raw files and rebuild when real-phone captures are available.
- The closed palettes are used for authored fills/strokes. Raster antialiasing, the requested soft device shadows, the explicit mid-grey icon review sheet, the unchanged official logo, and the original screenshot pixels necessarily contain additional raster colours. Background arcs use the exact palette colours with thin strokes, rather than adding translucent blue/cyan tints.
- The KRY feature graphic uses all three large ring arcs. The KRY icons use the brief's allowed single cyan ring because three frame rings crowded the 48 px marks. The official logo appears only in the presented-by badge, unchanged apart from proportional scaling.
- Following the additional request for polished icons, concept 1 was simplified from a double zigzag to one heartbeat flowing into the tick. Phone/lens overlaps have deliberate negative-space separation; unnecessary home-button decoration was removed. These are authored geometric SVG paths, not raster-generated icon artwork.
- Rebuild icons with `python3 docs/design/store-art/tools/build_brand.py`, features with `python3 docs/design/store-art/tools/build_features.py`, and screenshots with `python3 docs/design/store-art/tools/compose_screenshots.py`. The original verifier was not changed.
