# Icon review

## 1 — Pulse to tick
After the 48 px review and the request for a more polished mark, I removed the extra zigzag and home-button detail, joined the single heartbeat directly into the tick, and cut clear space into the phone at both crossings. I would ship this concept because its strong, uninterrupted rising stroke communicates the passing verdict fastest and remains distinct in monochrome.

## 2 — The ring
After the 48 px review I narrowed and optically repositioned the solid phone, retaining one bottom aperture so it reads as a handset, with a compact terminal tick and no percentage or segments. I would ship concept 1 instead because its verdict reads more directly, while this option deliberately recalls the app's score ring.

## 3 — Found it
After the 48 px review I removed the home-button detail and created a deliberate negative-space clearance around the lens so the phone and inspection mark do not merge into a heavy blob. I would ship concept 1 instead because this lens communicates inspection clearly but does not promise a passing outcome as immediately.

The three KRY frame rings crowded the small silhouettes, so the final exports use the brief's permitted single cyan ring without shrinking the art. The sheet includes true 512, 128 and 48 px exports plus circle, squircle and rounded-square masks; the revised six drawings were reviewed again at those sizes.
